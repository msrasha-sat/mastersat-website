The SAT Reading & Writing section is not a vocabulary test, and it isn't won by drilling random questions. It rewards a specific kind of literacy — one built from grammar that actually makes sense to you, rhetorical patterns you can name on sight, and reading habits trained for short, dense passages under time pressure.

This course is built around that reality. The sequence begins with clauses and complete sentences, then layers in punctuation, transitions, evidence questions, and rhetorical synthesis in a deliberate order — each session opens with what the previous one closed.

Every lesson uses only official College Board sources: Bluebook, the Question Bank, Khan Academy, released paper tests, and the Assessment Framework. No imitations. No worksheets pulled from the wrong test.

The Course

Three tiers of practice, woven together.

Homework isn't an afterthought — it's the engine of the course. Each tier serves a different purpose, and together they convert classroom understanding into test-day fluency.

i.

Topic Practice

Concentrated drills on the specific skill from each session — punctuation, subject-verb agreement, evidence selection — so the technique consolidates before it fades.

After every session
ii.

Cumulative Review

Mixed problem sets that interleave new material with everything covered so far. Builds the recall and pattern-recognition the real test demands.

Weekly
iii.

Test Prep Checkpoints

Timed sections under exam conditions. A diagnostic at session one, a targeted checkpoint mid-course, and a full Bluebook adaptive test at session eighteen.

Three milestones
Course Architecture

A sequence that compounds.

Most prep courses dump topics in textbook order. This one moves in the order students actually need them — building blocks first, synthesis last.

Phase 01 · Sessions 1–4

Foundations

Diagnostic Bluebook test. Clauses, complete sentences, the structural grammar that underlies every Writing question.

Phase 02 · Sessions 5–9

Standard English Conventions

Punctuation in earnest — semicolons, colons, dashes, commas — alongside agreement, modifiers, and verb forms.

Phase 03 · Sessions 10–13

Expression of Ideas

Transitions, rhetorical synthesis, and the writing-purpose questions. Checkpoint quiz on full Writing skillset.

Phase 04 · Sessions 14–17

Reading & Information

Command of evidence, cross-text connections, central ideas, vocabulary in context. Reading and writing become one toolkit.

Phase 05 · Session 18

Full Practice Test

Final Bluebook adaptive test with detailed score review and a personalized push-plan for test day.
